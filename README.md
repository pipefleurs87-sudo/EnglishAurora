**English Aurora** is an interactive, full-stack pedagogical English learning universe and teaching platform. It bridges high-engagement gamified 2.5D mechanics (guided by **Aurora**, the cosmic commander) with a rigorous CEFR curriculum spanning **85 comprehensive sequences** from **A1 (Breakthrough) to C1 (Mastery)**.

## 🌟 Key Highlights & Ecosystem

### 1. 🌌 The Interactive Galaxy (`index.html`)
A 2.5D procedural star map where each star represents a fully mastered lesson. Students navigate through constellations of grammar concepts, unlock Aurora Essence (XP), and access lessons directly.

### 2. 📖 Complete Pedagogical Triads (Zero-L1 & Pragmatic Rules)
No isolated worksheets. Each topic follows a strict cognitive progression:
- **Diagnostic Trap:** Contrasts the typical Spanish speaker confusion against authentic English.
- **Pragmatic Rule:** A one-sentence, use-case definition guided by our recurring characters (**Sofía Reyes**, **Ben Whitfield**, **Kenji Sato**, and **Abuela Carmen**).
- **Cognitive Triad:** Recognition (`multiple_choice`, `true_false`) → Manipulation (`gap_fill`, `unscramble`, `correct_mistake`) → Production / Transformation (`transformation`, `write_opposite`, `short_answer`).
- **Native Neural Audio:** Powered by humanized studio neural voices (`en-US-AriaNeural`, `en-GB-SoniaNeural`, `en-GB-RyanNeural`, `en-US-GuyNeural`, `en-US-AvaNeural`).
- **Answer Key & PDF Worksheet:** Instant print-ready worksheets with complete answer keys.

### 3. 🎮 Celestial Arcade Ludoteca (17 Educational Games)
A full suite of zero-L1 sensory games designed for spaced repetition and phonetic discrimination:
- **Vocabulary & Word Blitz:** *Meteor Words*, *Constellation Match*, *Gravity Sort*, *Rapid Eclipse*, *Celestial Riddles*, *Astral Wordle*, *Star Saver (Hangman)*, *Cosmic Word Search*.
- **Listening & Phonetics:** *Signal Hunter (Radar)*, *Sound Catch (Minimal Pairs)*, *Echo Chamber (Intonation Curves)*, *Pilot's Cabin (C1 Spatial Directives)*.
- **Grammar Quests:** *Unscramble the Sentence*, *Arcane Duel (Boss Battles)*, *Rune Shifter*, *Void Breaker*.

### 4. ⚔️ Cambridge & Exam Preparation
- **Cambridge Suite:** Syllabi, checklists, and targeted tests for **A2 Key (KET)**, **B1 Preliminary (PET)**, **B2 First (FCE)**, and **C1 Advanced (CAE)**.
- **Grammar Battles:** Deep dives into the 6 greatest dilemmas (*Past Simple vs Present Perfect*, *Will vs Going To*, *Make vs Do*, *Used To*, *Since vs For*, *Much vs Many*).
- **Irregular Verbs Master Matrix:** Phonetic and orthographic patterns for irregular verbs with instant audio playback.

### 5. 🔭 Teacher's Hub & Management Cockpit
- Real-time student cohorts, assignment dispatcher, live attendance ledger, and CEFR diagnostic radar.
- Direct sync with student accounts via Firebase Firestore (`?t=TOKEN`).
