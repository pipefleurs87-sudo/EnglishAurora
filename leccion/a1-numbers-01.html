Numbers — Counting, Age & Time

A1 — grammar, vocabulary, listening, reading, writing

Common mistake: “I have 25 years old.” — Correct: “I am 25 years old.”

We use numbers to count objects, tell our age with the verb 'to be', give phone numbers digit by digit, and say prices.

Practice

  1. Sofía ___ 28 years old. (has, is, have)
  2. Be careful with spelling: the number 40 is written ___. (forty, fourty, fourtee)
  3. The number 15 is pronounced with stress at the end: fif-___. (teen, ty, th)
  4. In English, we say telephone numbers digit by digit, and 0 is often pronounced 'oh'.
  5. It is correct to say: 'My brother has 18 years.'
  6. Ben is twenty-six ___ (years) old.
  7. Ten plus five is ___ (fifteen / write the word).
  8. There are ___ (twelve / 12) months in one year.
  9. Unscramble: am, I, twenty, years, old, .
  10. Unscramble: has, Kenji, three, notebooks, .
  11. Find the mistake: She have twenty years.
  12. Find the mistake: There are fourty students in the hall.
  13. Ben is twenty-six years old. — Turn it into a question asking for his age.
  14. I am thirty years old. — Make it negative (use 'not').
  15. Are you eighteen years old? (Short answer: Yes)
  16. Is room number forty-two on this floor? (Short answer: No)

Listening

Sofía: Welcome to Medellín, Ben! How was your flight?

Ben: It was great, but long! It took thirteen hours.

Sofía: Wow, thirteen hours! Are you staying in room forty-two?

Ben: Yes, room forty-two on the fourth floor.

Kenji: Hi guys! My new phone number is three-one-zero, five-five-five, nine-eight-seven.

Sofía: Perfect, Kenji! Let's save all our numbers.

Reading

Kenji and Ben are planning a road trip with Sofía. They need three bus tickets, two hotel rooms, and five bottles of water. Ben is twenty-six years old, Kenji is twenty-two, and Sofía is twenty-eight. The total cost of the bus tickets is ninety dollars: thirty dollars for each person. They are excited because their tour starts at seven in the morning.

  • Ben is older than Sofía.
  • The three friends need five bottles of water.
  • Each bus ticket costs thirty dollars.
  • The tour starts at nine in the morning.
