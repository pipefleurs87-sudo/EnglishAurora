Present Simple — Daily Routines · Ola B: La Rutina Nocturna de Ben

A1 — grammar, vocabulary, listening, reading, writing

Ben: I wake up at eleven. Sofía starts work at eight. We live in different worlds.

Sofía: Ben doesn't have a morning. He has a long afternoon.

Kenji: Does he really work at night?

Abuela Carmen: The night has its own workers. My Ben takes photos of the moon.

Common mistake: “Ben wake up late every day.” — Correct: “Ben wakes up late every day.”

We use the present simple to talk about routines and things we always do; with he/she/it the verb takes an '-s' at the end.

The third-person '-s'
SubjectVerbExample
I / You / We / Theywake upThey wake up late
He / She / Itwakes upBen wakes up late
Negative / Questiondo / doesDoes he work at night?
  • 🌙 wake up late — despertarse tarde
  • 📷 take photos — tomar fotos
  • 💻 edit photos — editar fotos
  • 🍜 have lunch — almorzar
  • 🚶 go out — salir
  • 🏠 come back — volver
  • 📧 check email — revisar el correo
  • 😴 stay up — quedarse despierto
  • Ben wakes up late and takes photos at sunset.
  • He edits photos at night and checks his email.
  • His camera sleeps more than he does.
  • Ben does not wake up early.
  • He doesn't work in the morning.
  • Does Ben edit photos every night?
  • What time does he have lunch?
  • Does he take photos at sunset? Yes, he does.
  • Do you stay up late? No, I don't.

Practice

  1. Ben ___ up at eleven o'clock. (wake, wakes, waking)
  2. ___ Ben work at night? (Do, Does, Is)
  3. He ___ photos at sunset. (take, takes, taking)
  4. With he/she/it, we add '-s' to the verb: 'Ben wakes up late'.
  5. The correct question is: 'Does Ben wakes up late?'
  6. Ben ___ photos at night. (edit)
  7. He ___ not wake up early.
  8. Ben ___ his email after lunch. (check)
  9. Unscramble: Ben, takes, photos, at, sunset, .
  10. Unscramble: does, he, work, at, night
  11. Find the mistake: Ben wake up at eleven.
  12. Find the mistake: He don't work in the morning.
  13. Find the mistake: Does Ben edits photos at night?
  14. Ben edits photos every night. — Make it negative (use 'does not').
  15. Ben wakes up late. — Turn it into a question (use 'does').
  16. Does he take photos at sunset? — Answer with a short answer (Yes).
  17. Ben works in the morning. — Write the negative form.
  18. What time do you have lunch? (Answer with a full sentence.)

Listening

Sofía: What does Ben do every day?

Kenji: He wakes up at eleven and has lunch at two.

Sofía: And then?

Kenji: He takes photos at sunset and edits them at night.

Sofía: Does he ever sleep?

Kenji: Yes, he does. He sleeps from four to eleven.

Reading

Ben is a photographer, and his routine is upside down. He wakes up at eleven and has lunch at two. In the afternoon, he walks around the city with his camera. He takes photos at sunset, when the light is golden. At night, he edits photos and checks his email. He does not work in the morning — the morning does not exist for Ben. He goes to bed at four. His friends say he is a bat with a camera.

  • Ben wakes up at eleven.
  • He takes photos in the morning.
  • Ben edits photos at night.
  • He goes to bed at ten.
