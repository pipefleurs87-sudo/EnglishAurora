Articles — a / an / the · Ola C: El Viaje del Equipaje Perdido

A1 — grammar, vocabulary, listening, reading, writing

Kenji: I have a ticket, a suitcase and an hour before the plane.

Ben: I have a backpack and a map. The map is from 1998, but it is a beautiful map.

Sofía: The taxi is at the door. Don't lose the ticket, Ben.

Abuela Carmen: A traveler without a plan finds an adventure. The adventure finds the rest.

Common mistake: “I have ticket and suitcase.” — Correct: “I have a ticket and a suitcase.”

Articles present a noun: a/an for one thing among many (any taxi), the for the specific one in the story (the plane we must catch) — and English needs them even where Spanish doesn't.

a / an / the — en el viaje
ArticleUse it for...Example
aone thing — before consonant SOUNDa ticket, a map, a hotel
anone thing — before vowel SOUNDan hour, an old backpack, an airport
thethe specific one in the storythe plane, the door, the ticket
a + jobEnglish needs it: She is A pilotHe is an engineer
no articlegeneralizations in pluralI like maps (not 'the maps')

Practice

  1. Kenji has ___ ticket and ___ suitcase. (a / a, an / a, the / an)
  2. The plane leaves in ___ hour. (a, an, the)
  3. ___ taxi is at the door. (A, An, The)
  4. She is ___ pilot. (a, an, —)
  5. We say 'an hour' because the 'h' is silent: the SOUND is a vowel.
  6. You can say 'I have ticket' without an article in English.
  7. There is ___ taxi in front of the hotel. (a / an / the)
  8. Ben carries ___ old backpack. (a / an / the)
  9. ___ plane leaves in an hour. (a / an / the)
  10. Kenji doesn't have ___ map of the city. (a / an / the)
  11. Unscramble: is, there, a, hotel, near, the, airport
  12. Unscramble: the, taxi, is, at, the, door
  13. Find the mistake: I have a hour before the plane.
  14. Find the mistake: She is pilot of the plane.
  15. Find the mistake: A ticket is in my bag, the blue one.
  16. Kenji has a map. — Write the negative form.
  17. There is a hotel near the airport. — Write the yes/no question.
  18. Is the plane on time? — Answer with a short answer (No).
  19. Ben has the ticket. — Write the negative form.
  20. Do you have a map? (short answer, Yes)

Listening

Kenji: Ben, do you have the ticket?

Ben: Yes... no. Wait. I have a map, a sandwich and an old backpack.

Kenji: Ben! The plane leaves in an hour!

Ben: Relax. There is a taxi at the door and a ticket in my pocket.

Kenji: Which pocket?

Ben: That is an excellent question.

Reading

Kenji writes in his diary: 'Today Ben almost lost the ticket. We arrived at the airport with an hour to spare. There was a long line at the door, and a dog slept next to a suitcase — an enormous suitcase with a map on top. Ben talked to a pilot about the weather. The pilot said the plane was on time. In the end, the ticket was in Ben's pocket the whole time. It is always an adventure with Ben. Next week: the hotel. I am afraid.'

  • Ben lost the ticket forever.
  • A dog slept next to a suitcase.
  • The pilot said the plane was late.
  • The ticket was in Ben's pocket.
