Articles — a / an / the · Ola B: El Café de las Runas

A1 — grammar, vocabulary, listening, reading, writing

Ben: I need a coffee. A big coffee. Today I am an exhausted photographer.

Kenji: Look, there is an old piano in the corner! And a cat under the table.

Sofía: The menu is on the wall. They have an excellent croissant.

Abuela Carmen: In a café, the first rule is: never rush the coffee.

Common mistake: “I need coffee, please. A table for one.” — Correct: “I need a coffee, please. A table for one.”

Articles present a noun: a/an for one thing among many (any table), the for the specific one everybody in the scene knows (the bill) — and English needs them even where Spanish doesn't.

Practice

  1. Ben orders ___ coffee and ___ croissant. (a / a, an / a, the / an)
  2. There is ___ old piano in the corner. (a, an, the)
  3. ___ bill is on the table. (A, An, The)
  4. He is ___ waiter at the café. (a, an, —)
  5. We say 'an umbrella' because 'umbrella' starts with a vowel SOUND.
  6. In English you can say 'He is waiter' without an article.
  7. Kenji sits at ___ table next to the window. (a / an / the)
  8. There is ___ umbrella under the chair. (a / an / the)
  9. The café has ___ cat. It sleeps under the piano. (a / an / the)
  10. ___ menu is on the wall. (a / an / the)

Listening

Ben: Excuse me, is there a table near the window?

Sofía: Yes, the table in the corner is free.

Ben: Perfect. A coffee and a croissant, please.

Kenji: Look, Ben! There is a cat under your chair.

Ben: A cat? It is an elegant cat. It has a hat.

Sofía: That is the café cat. The hat is an accident.

Reading

Ben writes on his blog: 'There is a café near the museum, and it is my office now. The café has an old piano, a sleepy cat and the best croissant in the city. I sit at the same table every day — the table next to the window. Today a waiter dropped a cup, and the cat didn't move. It is a very calm cat. I ordered the bill an hour ago. I am still waiting. It is a slow café, but it is my café.'
