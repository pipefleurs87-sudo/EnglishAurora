Past Perfect — The Timeline Detective

B1 — grammar, reading, listening, writing

Sofía: When I arrived at the platform, the train had already left!

Ben: Did you run? Or had you fallen asleep at the cafe?

Kenji: I realized that she had forgotten to check the schedule earlier that morning.

Abuela Carmen: In my day, if you had missed the train, you simply walked under the stars!

Common mistake: “When I arrived, the film already started.” — Correct: “When I arrived, the film had already started.”

The Past Perfect shows the 'past of the past' — the earlier of two completed actions in the timeline.

Timeline Detective: Which event happened first?

Practice

  1. When Sofía arrived at the station, the train ___ (had already left., has already left., already lefted.)
  2. Kenji realized that he ___ his keys at home. (had forgotten, has forgotten, forgotting)
  3. By the time the guests arrived, Abuela Carmen ___ dinner. (had cooked, has cooked, was cooked)
  4. She couldn't buy the ticket because she ___ her wallet. (had lost, has lost, losted)
  5. Had you visited London before last summer? ___ (Yes, I had., Yes, I have., Yes, I did.)
  6. The Past Perfect is formed with 'had' + the past participle (3rd column of irregular verbs).
  7. In a narrative, the Past Perfect is used for the action that happened later.
  8. She ___ (not / lock) the studio door before leaving.
  9. When we woke up, Ben ___ (already / prepare) breakfast.
  10. They ___ (never / see) the northern lights before that trip.
  11. Unscramble: the, train, had, already, left, when, we, arrived
  12. Unscramble: had, you, ever, seen, the, northern, lights, before
  13. Find the mistake: When I arrived, the film already started.
  14. Find the mistake: She had forgot her keys at the cafe.
  15. Sofía arrived. Before that, the train left. — Join the sentences using 'when' and Past Perfect.
  16. She finished her tea. Then she walked out into the snow. — Join using 'before' and Past Perfect.
  17. They had finished the report by midnight. — Write the sentence in negative.
  18. Had you finished the task before 5 PM? (Short answer: Yes)
  19. Had they locked the front door before leaving? (Short answer: No)

Listening

Sofía: Kenji, why did you arrive so late to the rehearsal?

Kenji: By the time my bus arrived, the road **had flooded** completely.

Ben: But had you checked the weather forecast before leaving?

Kenji: No, I hadn't. I **had assumed** it was going to be a sunny day.

Abuela Carmen: Next time, listen to the wind! I **had noticed** the storm clouds before sunrise.

Reading

The old clock tower in the village square had stood silently for fifty years. But when the travelers reached the peak of the mountain at midnight, they noticed something extraordinary: the bells had begun to ring. Local legends claimed that ancient guardians had hidden a secret parchment inside the mechanical gears. Sofia and Ben ran towards the tower doors, only to discover that someone had already opened the bronze lock. The adventure had officially begun.

  • The clock tower had been silent for fifty years before that night.
  • The bells started ringing before the travelers reached the mountain peak.
  • Someone had already unlocked the bronze door before Sofia and Ben arrived.
  • The guardians had hidden a map under the village bridge.
