Indefinite Pronouns — Someone, Anyone, No One, Everyone

B1 — grammar, vocabulary, listening, speaking

Sofía: Somebody left their notebook on my desk!

Ben: Is there anything written inside it?

Kenji: Nobody touched it. I think everyone was at the library.

Abuela Carmen: Look closely! If there is nothing inside, it belongs to the future.

Common mistake: “Everybody are ready for the exam.” — Correct: “Everybody is ready for the exam.”

Indefinite pronouns refer to non-specific people, things, or places. In English, they always take a singular verb.

Practice

  1. Everybody ___ ready to begin the lesson. (is, are, were)
  2. I looked for my keys, but they were ___ to be found. (nowhere, anywhere, somewhere)
  3. Did ___ call while I was out? (anybody, somebody, nobody)
  4. There is ___ interesting in this magazine. (nothing, nobody, nowhere)
  5. Would you like ___ to drink? (Polite offer) (something, anything, nothing)
  6. Words like 'everyone', 'somebody', and 'nothing' take a singular verb.
  7. In standard English, double negatives like 'I don't know nobody' are correct.
  8. I opened the fridge, but there was ___ (nothing / anything) left.
  9. Does ___ (anyone / someone) have a pencil I could borrow?
  10. She packed her bags and went ___ (somewhere / anywhere) peaceful.
  11. Unscramble: everybody, knows, the, secret, in, town
  12. Unscramble: did, you, find, anything, interesting, there
  13. Find the mistake: Everybody are happy today.
  14. Find the mistake: I didn't see nobody at the party.
  15. There wasn't anyone in the room. — Rewrite using 'no one'.
  16. I saw someone in the garden. — Rewrite in the negative form using 'anyone'.
  17. Everything went wrong during the rehearsal. — Write the opposite using 'nothing'.
  18. Is there anything in the bag? (Short answer: No)
  19. Did anyone find the missing map? (Short answer: Yes)

Listening

Sofía: Has **anyone** seen my blue sketchbook?

Ben: I searched **everywhere**, but I couldn't find **anything**.

Kenji: Look under the table! **Somebody** placed a stack of papers there.

Sofía: Ah, here it is! **Nothing** is lost after all.

Abuela Carmen: Remember, my dear: **everyone** finds what they seek when they look with calm eyes.

Reading

In the whispering forest of Whispering Pines, everyone knows the legend of the Star Stone. The tale says that someone placed a glowing gem inside an ancient oak tree three hundred years ago. Many adventurers have searched everywhere across the valley, but nobody has ever discovered its hiding place. Some believe there is nothing true in the old story, while others say that anyone with a pure heart can hear the stone humming in the twilight.

  • Everyone in Whispering Pines knows the legend of the Star Stone.
  • Someone found the stone last year.
  • Many adventurers have searched across the valley.
  • The legend claims the stone was hidden in a deep cave.
