Present Simple — Daily Routines

A1 — grammar, vocabulary, listening, reading, writing

Common mistake: “Sofía work in Medellín.” — Correct: “Sofía works in Medellín.”

We use the present simple to talk about routines and things we always do; with he/she/it the verb takes an '-s' at the end.

The third-person '-s'
SubjectVerbExample
I / You / We / TheyworkThey work
He / She / ItworksShe works
Negative / Questiondo / doesDoes she work?
  • wake up — despertarse
  • have breakfast — desayunar
  • go to work — ir al trabajo
  • start / finish — empezar / terminar
  • get home — llegar a casa
  • cook dinner — cocinar la cena
  • go to bed — irse a dormir
  • work / works — trabaja
  • Sofía wakes up at six and works in Medellín.
  • She has breakfast and goes to work.
  • Her brother starts work at nine.
  • Sofía does not work on Sundays.
  • She doesn't watch TV in the morning.
  • Does Sofía cook dinner every night?
  • What time do you get home?
  • Does she work in Medellín? Yes, she does.
  • Do you wake up early? No, I don't.

Practice

  1. Sofía ___ up at six o'clock. (wake, wakes, waking)
  2. ___ she work in Medellín? (Do, Does, Is)
  3. With he/she/it, we add '-s' to the verb in the present simple.
  4. She ___ breakfast before she goes to work. (have)
  5. Sofía ___ not work on Sundays.
  6. Unscramble: work, Sofía, at, starts, eight, .
  7. Find the mistake: She go to work at seven.
  8. Sofía cooks dinner every night. — Make it negative (use 'does not').
  9. Sofía wakes up early. — Turn it into a question (use 'does').
  10. What time do you wake up? (Answer with a full sentence.)

Listening

Kenji: What does Sofía do every day?

Ben: She wakes up at six and has breakfast.

Kenji: And then?

Ben: She goes to work. She starts at eight.

Kenji: Does she cook dinner?

Ben: Yes, she does. Then she goes to bed early.

Reading

Sofía is a designer in Medellín. Every day she wakes up at six o'clock. She has breakfast, and then she goes to work. She starts at eight and finishes at five. In the evening, she cooks dinner and goes to bed early. She does not work on Sundays; she rests and visits her family.

  • Sofía wakes up at six o'clock.
  • She finishes work at eight.
  • Sofía does not work on Sundays.
