Relative Clauses — Places in the City (a place where...)

A1 — grammar, vocabulary, listening, reading, writing

Common mistake: “A hospital is a place which people get better.” — Correct: “A hospital is a place where people get better.”

We use 'where' to define a place by what you do or what happens there: 'a place where + subject + verb'.

a place where + action

  • A library is a place where you read and study.
  • A gym is a place where people do exercise.
  • A bank is not a place where you buy food.
  • A park isn't a place where you sleep.

Reading

Medellín is a city with many places. A library is a place where you read and study, and a park is a place where you relax. Sofía loves the gym, a place where she does exercise every week. Ben prefers the cinema, a place where you watch films. 'What is your favourite place?' asks Kenji. 'The airport,' says Ben, 'because it's a place where new adventures begin!'
