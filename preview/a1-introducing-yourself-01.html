Introducing Yourself — Greetings & Personal Information

A1 — grammar, vocabulary, listening, reading, writing, speaking

Common mistake: “Hello, I call myself Ben and I have 24 years.” — Correct: “Hello, my name is Ben and I am 24 years old.”

We introduce ourselves to share our name, origin, age, and occupation when connecting with someone for the first time.

Practice

  1. When someone says 'Nice to meet you!', the most natural reply is: ___ (Nice to meet you too!, Equally!, I call myself too!)
  2. To state your occupation correctly, you say: 'I am ___ architect.' (an, a, the)
  3. Where ___ you from? (are, is, am)
  4. In English, it is natural to introduce yourself by saying: 'I call myself John.'
  5. When introducing yourself, 'I am Sofía' and 'My name is Sofía' are both correct and natural.
  6. Hello! My ___ (name / write the word) is Kenji.
  7. I am from Japan, but I ___ (live) in Medellín now.
  8. Sofía is ___ (a / an) graphic designer.
  9. Unscramble: name, My, is, Kenji, Sato, .
  10. Unscramble: meet, to, Nice, you, too, !
  11. Find the mistake: Hello, I call myself Carlos.
  12. Find the mistake: I am from Colombia and I am designer.
  13. My name is Ben. — Turn it into a question asking for someone's name.
  14. I am an exchange student. — Make it negative (use 'not').
  15. Are you from Colombia? (Short answer: Yes)
  16. Is your name Sofía? (Short answer: Yes)

Listening

Kenji: Good morning! Is this seat taken?

Sofía: No, it is free. Please, sit down! I am Sofía.

Kenji: Nice to meet you, Sofía. My name is Kenji.

Sofía: Nice to meet you too! Where are you from, Kenji?

Kenji: I am from Japan, from Tokyo. What about you?

Sofía: I am from Medellín. I am a graphic designer here.

Reading

Today is the first day of the language exchange club in Medellín. Sofía welcomes the new members. Kenji introduces himself first: he is twenty-two years old and studies international business. Next is Ben, a twenty-six-year-old travel writer from London who loves South American music. Finally, Sofía introduces herself: she is twenty-eight, works as a designer, and loves showing her city to visitors. Everyone feels welcome and ready to learn.

  • Kenji studies international business.
  • Ben is from the United States.
  • Sofía is twenty-eight years old.
  • The club is meeting in London.
