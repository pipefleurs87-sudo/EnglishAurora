Greetings & Farewells — Daily Salutations & Partings

A1 — vocabulary, speaking, listening, reading, writing, grammar

Sofía: Good morning, Ben! How are you doing today?

Ben: Good morning, Sofía! I am doing well, thanks. How is it going with the studio?

Sofía: Fantastic! Look at the time, I must go to my meeting. See you later!

Ben: Have a nice day, Sofía! Take care!

Common mistake: “Good night everyone! I just arrived at the evening class, how are you?” — Correct: “Good evening everyone! I just arrived at the evening class, how are you?”

We use greetings to open social interactions politely and farewells to close them warmly depending on the time of day and formality.

  3. It is Friday at 5:00 PM and everyone is leaving work. You say: ___ (Have a great weekend!, Good morning!, Pleased to meet you!)
  4. You say goodbye to a classmate you will see again the next morning: ___ (See you tomorrow!, Until the morning!, Good evening!)
  5. 'Good night' is a greeting used when you enter a room in the evening.
  6. 'See you later' and 'Take care' are natural ways to say goodbye to a friend.

Listening

Sofía: Good morning, Kenji! How is your English class going?

Kenji: Good morning, Sofía! It is going great. How are you doing today?

Sofía: I am doing very well, thanks! Ben is joining us for coffee this afternoon.

Kenji: Wonderful! What time should we meet at the cafe?

Sofía: Let us meet at three o'clock. See you later!

Kenji: See you later, Sofía! Have a nice afternoon!

Reading

Every morning at eight o'clock, Sofía arrives at her design studio in Medellín and says, 'Good morning everyone!' Her colleagues reply warmly, 'Morning, Sofía!' During the day, clients visit the studio, and Sofía always greets them politely with, 'Good afternoon, welcome to our studio.' When leaving the office in the late afternoon, she never says 'good night' to people who are still arriving. Instead, she smiles and says, 'See you tomorrow, take care!' In English, choosing the right greeting for each moment of the day builds trust and strong relationships.

  • Sofía arrives at her design studio in the morning.
  • Sofía says 'Good night' to clients when they arrive in the afternoon.
  • Sofía says 'See you tomorrow, take care!' when leaving the office.
  • In English, greetings change depending on the time of day.
