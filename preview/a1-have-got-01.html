Have got — Possessions

A1 — grammar, vocabulary, listening, reading, writing

Ben: In Britain we say it like this: I've got a camera, I've got a plan, and I haven't got any money.

Sofía: So 'have got' = 'have'? I have a laptop... and I've got a laptop?

Ben: Exactly! Same meaning. But careful: Have you got...? — no 'do' in the question!

Abuela Carmen: I've got four grandchildren, two hands and one rule. Has anybody got questions? No? Dinner time.

Common mistake: “Do you have got a pen?” — Correct: “Have you got a pen?”

'Have got' says what you own or possess — it means the same as 'have', but makes questions and negatives without 'do': Have you got...? I haven't got...

Practice

  1. Sofía ___ got a laptop. (has, have, is)
  2. ___ you got a pen? (Do, Have, Are)
  3. I ___ got an umbrella, and it's raining! (haven't, don't, hasn't)
  4. 'Have got' means the same as 'have': I've got a bike = I have a bike.
  5. The question with 'have got' uses 'do': 'Do you have got a pen?'
  6. Kenji ___ got a guitar, but he wants one. (negative)
  7. We ___ got a big family.
  8. ___ she got a dog?
  9. Unscramble: I, have, got, a, bike
  10. Unscramble: has, she, got, a, dog
  11. Find the mistake: Do you have got a pen?
  12. Find the mistake: She have got two cameras.
  13. Find the mistake: I don't got my keys.
  14. Ben has got his keys. — Write the negative form.
  15. They have got a camera. — Write the yes/no question.
  16. Have you got a bike? — Answer with a short answer (Yes).
  17. I have got a car. — Write the negative form.
  18. Kenji hasn't got a guitar. — Write the affirmative form.
  19. Have you got a phone? (short answer)
  20. Has your teacher got a helicopter? (short answer, No)

Listening

Sofía: Trip checklist! Ben, have you got the tickets?

Ben: Yes, I have! And I've got my camera and my hat.

Sofía: Kenji, have you got the map?

Kenji: Yes! And I've got snacks for everybody.

Sofía: Perfect. Ben... have you got your keys?

Ben: ...I haven't got my keys. One moment, please.

Reading

Ben's British corner, episode one: 'Have got'. In London, my family says it all the time. My mum has got a garden with roses. My dad has got an old car — older than me. My sister has got three cats, and the cats have got strong opinions. Me? I've got a camera, a backpack, and a one-way ticket to adventure. I haven't got a lot of money, but I've got good friends here in Colombia — and Abuela Carmen says that a person with good friends has got everything.
